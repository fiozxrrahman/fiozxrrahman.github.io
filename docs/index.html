Introduction

I'm FIOZUR RAHMAN — a developer who builds across the stack. My work sits at the intersection of web development, AI systems, automation, networking and open-source software.

I care about building software that actually works, is simple to use, and solves real problems. I learn by building, and I share what I build whenever I can.

Outside of client work, I spend time experimenting with new tools, contributing to open-source, and exploring how AI and automation can make developers more effective.
